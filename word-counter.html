What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online writing tool that measures the length of a piece of text. It helps you check word totals, character totals, sentences and paragraphs without manually counting them.

Students can use a word counter for essays and assignments. Writers, teachers, bloggers and content creators can use it to check article length, prepare drafts and meet writing requirements.

How to Use This Word Counter

  1. Type your text or paste it into the text box.
  2. View the word, character, sentence and paragraph totals.
  3. Check the estimated reading and speaking times.
  4. Choose Copy Text to copy your text.
  5. Choose Download Text to save it as a text file.
  6. Choose Clear Text to start again.

How Are Words and Characters Counted?

Words: Groups of non-whitespace characters are counted as words. Groups separated by spaces or line breaks are counted separately.

Characters: The character total includes spaces, punctuation and line breaks. Unicode grapheme clusters are counted so that many emoji and accented characters are treated as one visible character.

Characters without spaces: This count excludes whitespace, including spaces, tabs and line breaks.

Sentences: Sentence totals are estimates based on sentence-ending punctuation and browser sentence segmentation where available. Abbreviations and unusual punctuation can affect the result.

Paragraphs: A paragraph is counted as a non-empty block of text separated from another block by one or more blank lines.

Reading Time and Speaking Time

Reading time is estimated using an average reading speed of 200 words per minute. Speaking time uses an average of 130 words per minute. Actual times vary depending on the reader, language, content and speaking speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes. You can use this word counter without paying or creating an account.

Does the word count update automatically?

Yes. The totals update as you type, paste, edit or remove text.

Are spaces included in the character count?

Yes. The main character total includes spaces, punctuation and line breaks. A separate result shows characters without whitespace.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute. It is a general estimate, not a measurement of your actual reading speed.

Can I download my text?

Yes. Select Download Text to save the text you entered as a plain text file on your device.

Is my text uploaded when I count words?

The counting JavaScript on this page processes your text locally in your browser and does not submit it to a server. Avoid entering sensitive information on shared or untrusted devices.
