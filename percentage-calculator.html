What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred." Percentages are commonly used in mathematics, finance, shopping, statistics, business and everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100.

For example, 20% of 500 is:

20 × 500 ÷ 100 = 100

Percentage Increase Formula

Percentage increase can be calculated using the following formula:

Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Percentage Decrease Formula

Percentage decrease can be calculated using:

Percentage Decrease = ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100
