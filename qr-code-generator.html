What Is a QR Code?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a two-dimensional barcode that stores information. A smartphone camera or QR scanner can read it to open a website, display text, prepare an email or perform other supported actions.

Where Can You Use QR Codes?

  • Website links and business pages.
  • Business cards and contact materials.
  • Restaurant menus and product information.
  • Posters, brochures and advertisements.
  • WhatsApp contact and message links.
  • Educational resources and classroom materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. You can create and download QR codes without paying or registering.

2. Do QR codes expire?

QR codes generated by this tool are static. They do not have an automatic expiry date. However, a website or other destination encoded in a QR code may become unavailable.

3. Can I create a QR code for WhatsApp?

Yes. Enter a WhatsApp number with its country code and optionally add a pre-filled message.

4. Can I change QR code colors?

Yes. You can select a foreground color and background color before generating the QR code. Keep enough contrast for reliable scanning.

5. Are my details uploaded to a server?

This tool generates QR codes in your browser. The QR code content is processed locally, although the QR generation library is loaded from an external content delivery network.
