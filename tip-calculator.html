What Is a Tip Calculator?

A Tip Calculator helps you quickly calculate how much tip to leave based on your bill and chosen tip percentage. It can also divide the total bill equally between multiple people.

How to Calculate a Tip

The tip amount is calculated by multiplying the bill amount by the tip percentage and dividing by 100.

Tip Amount = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

The total bill is the original bill plus the tip.

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip Amount

Example

If your restaurant bill is $100 and you want to leave a 15% tip:

Tip = $100 × 15 ÷ 100 = $15

Total Bill = $100 + $15 = $115

If four people are sharing the bill, each person would pay $28.75.
