What Is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you determine how much to add to a bill based on a selected tip percentage. It can also divide the final bill equally among several people.

This makes it useful for restaurant bills, personal services, group meals, and other situations where you want to estimate a gratuity and split the total cost.

How to Calculate a Tip

The tip amount is calculated by multiplying the bill by the selected tip percentage and dividing by 100.

Tip Amount = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

The total bill is the original bill plus the calculated tip.

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip Amount

If the bill is shared equally, divide the total bill by the number of people.

Amount Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Tip Calculator Example

Suppose a restaurant bill is $100 and you choose a 15% tip.

The tip is: $100 × 15 ÷ 100 = $15.

The total bill is therefore $115. If four people share the bill equally, each person pays $28.75.

Common Tip Percentages

  • 10% – A lower gratuity in situations where appropriate
  • 15% – A commonly used percentage in some situations
  • 18% – A frequently used restaurant tip percentage
  • 20% – A common choice for good service
  • 25% – A higher gratuity for excellent service
Important: Tipping customs vary by country, industry, business, and service. Some locations include service charges or gratuity automatically. Check your bill before adding a tip.

Why Use a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator reduces manual calculations and makes it easier to estimate the final amount when paying a bill or sharing it with other people. You can quickly compare different tip percentages and see the resulting total.

Tip Calculator FAQs

How do I calculate a tip?

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage and divide by 100. For example, a 20% tip on a $50 bill is $10.

How much should I tip?

The appropriate amount depends on the country, type of service, local customs, and the quality of service. This calculator lets you enter the percentage you choose.

Can I split the bill between several people?

Yes. Enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator divides the total bill, including the tip, equally.

Does the calculator include the tip in the total bill?

Yes. The Total Bill result includes the original bill plus the calculated tip amount.

Can I use a custom tip percentage?

Yes. You can enter any valid tip percentage supported by the calculator instead of choosing a preset percentage.
