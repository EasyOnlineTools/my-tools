What Is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you quickly calculate how much tip to leave based on your bill amount and selected tip percentage.

It can also divide the total bill equally among multiple people, making it useful when dining at restaurants or sharing service costs with friends and family.

How to Calculate a Tip

Tip Amount = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

After calculating the tip, the total bill is calculated by adding the tip to the original bill.

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip Amount

When splitting the bill, the total bill is divided by the number of people.

Amount Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Example

Suppose your restaurant bill is $100 and you want to leave a 15% tip.

The tip would be $15, making the total bill $115.

If four people are sharing the bill, each person would pay $28.75.

Common Tip Percentages

  • 10% – Basic service tip
  • 15% – Common tip amount
  • 18% – Common restaurant tip
  • 20% – Frequently used for good service
  • 25% – Higher tip for excellent service

Appropriate tipping practices vary by country, industry, and situation. Use the percentage that fits your circumstances.

Tip Calculator FAQs

How do I calculate a tip?

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage and divide the result by 100. For example, a 20% tip on a $50 bill is $10.

How much should I tip?

Tip amounts vary depending on the country, service, and situation. You can enter any percentage into this calculator to calculate the corresponding tip.

Can I split the bill between several people?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator will show the estimated amount each person would pay when the bill is divided equally.

Does the calculator include the tip in the total bill?

Yes. The Total Bill result includes both the original bill and the calculated tip.
