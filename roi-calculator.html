What Is ROI?

ROI stands for Return on Investment. It is a percentage that shows how much profit or loss an investment has generated compared with the original amount invested.

ROI can be useful when comparing the performance of different investments, projects, purchases, or business decisions.

ROI Formula

ROI = ((Final Value − Initial Investment) ÷ Initial Investment) × 100

For example, if you invest 10,000 and the investment grows to 12,500, your profit is 2,500 and your ROI is 25%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does ROI mean?

ROI means Return on Investment. It measures the gain or loss from an investment relative to the original investment amount.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. If the final investment value is lower than the initial investment, the ROI will be negative, indicating a loss.

Does this calculator include investment time?

No. This basic ROI calculator calculates the total return based on the initial investment and final value. It does not calculate annualized ROI.
