Random Numbers

What is a Random Number Generator?

A random number generator creates numbers randomly within a range that you choose. You can specify a minimum number, maximum number and how many random numbers you want.

How to Use the Random Number Generator

Enter your minimum and maximum values, choose how many numbers you want, and click "Generate Random Numbers".

You can also choose whether repeated numbers are allowed or whether every generated number must be unique.
