What Is an Investment Calculator?

An investment calculator estimates how an initial investment and regular contributions could grow over time when an assumed annual rate of return is compounded.

It can help you understand the potential effect of compound growth, regular investing, and time on the future value of an investment.

Investment Growth Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)nt

Where A is the future value, P is the initial investment, r is the annual rate of return, n is the number of compounding periods per year, and t is the investment period in years.

Regular monthly contributions are also included in the calculation so you can estimate the effect of investing additional money over time.

Example Investment Calculation

Suppose you invest $10,000 initially, add $300 each month, and assume an annual return of 7% for 20 years.

The calculator estimates the future value by combining the initial investment, monthly contributions, and assumed compound growth.

Why Time Matters in Investing

Compound growth can become more significant over longer periods because investment returns can themselves generate additional returns. Regular contributions can also increase the amount of capital available to grow.

The result from this calculator is an estimate and does not guarantee future investment performance. Actual returns can vary, and investments may lose value.

Investment Calculator FAQs

What does an investment calculator calculate?

It estimates the future value of an investment using the initial investment, regular contributions, expected annual return, compounding frequency, and investment period.

Can I include monthly investments?

Yes. Enter your planned monthly contribution to include regular investments in the estimated future value.

What annual return should I enter?

Enter an assumed annual rate of return appropriate for the investment scenario you are evaluating. Actual investment returns can be higher or lower.

Does the calculator guarantee investment returns?

No. This calculator provides a mathematical estimate based on the values you enter. Actual investment performance is uncertain and investments can lose value.
