What Is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a numerical value calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening measure for weight categories in adults.

BMI Formula

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

Adult BMI Categories

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy Weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or above Obesity

What Is a BMI Calculator?

A BMI Calculator is a simple online tool that estimates Body Mass Index (BMI) using a person's height and weight. BMI is commonly used as a general screening measure to compare body weight with height.

How to Calculate BMI

BMI is calculated by dividing a person's weight in kilograms by the square of their height in meters.

BMI Formula: BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

For example, if someone weighs 70 kg and is 1.75 meters tall, their BMI is approximately 22.9.

BMI Categories

For adults, BMI is commonly grouped into the following categories:

  • Underweight: BMI below 18.5
  • Healthy weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25.0 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30.0 or higher

Who Can Use a BMI Calculator?

A BMI calculator can be useful for adults who want a quick estimate of their BMI. It can be used at home, at the gym or anywhere you have access to a web browser.

BMI is only a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat. Factors such as muscle mass, age and overall health can affect how BMI should be interpreted.

BMI Calculator FAQs

What does BMI mean?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a numerical value calculated from height and weight and is commonly used as a general screening measure.

Is BMI the same as body fat percentage?

No. BMI is calculated from height and weight, while body fat percentage estimates how much of the body is made up of fat.

Can BMI be used for everyone?

Standard adult BMI categories are not appropriate for every person or situation. Children, teenagers, pregnant people and some athletes may need different methods of assessment.
