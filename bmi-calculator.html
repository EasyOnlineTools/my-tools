What Is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a numerical value calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening measure for weight categories in adults.

BMI Formula

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

Adult BMI Categories

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy Weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or above Obesity

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a normal BMI?

For adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is generally categorized as a healthy weight range.

Is BMI the same for everyone?

BMI is a general screening measure and does not directly measure body fat. It may not accurately reflect health for every individual.
