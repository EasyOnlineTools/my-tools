What Is Body Mass Index (BMI)?

Body Mass Index, or BMI, is a number calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a screening measure to group adult weight relative to height. BMI does not directly measure body fat and cannot determine a person's overall health on its own.

BMI Formula

BMI can be calculated using metric or US customary measurements.

Metric formula: BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

US formula: BMI = 703 × Weight (lb) ÷ Height (in)²

Example: A person weighing 70 kg and measuring 175 cm tall has a BMI of approximately 22.9.

What Is a Healthy Weight Range?

For adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is commonly classified as the healthy-weight range. The calculator estimates the corresponding weight range for your entered height. This is a general screening reference, not an individual health recommendation.

Why Can BMI Be Limited?

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat or show where body fat is distributed. Age, muscle mass, pregnancy and other individual factors may affect how a BMI result should be interpreted. Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific BMI assessment rather than standard adult categories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI for adults?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly classified as the healthy-weight range for adults aged 20 and older. This is a screening range, not a guarantee of good health.

Can I calculate BMI in pounds and feet?

Yes. Select US Units, enter your weight in pounds and your height in feet and inches, then select Calculate BMI.

Does age affect BMI?

Standard adult BMI categories are generally used for adults aged 20 and older. Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific BMI interpretation.

Is BMI the same as body fat percentage?

No. BMI uses height and weight, while body fat percentage estimates the proportion of the body made up of fat. They measure different things.

Can this calculator be used for children?

This calculator displays standard adult BMI categories. Children and teenagers need a different interpretation based on age and sex. Ask a qualified healthcare professional for appropriate guidance.

Is this BMI calculator free?
