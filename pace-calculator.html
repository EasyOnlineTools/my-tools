What Is a Pace Calculator?

A Pace Calculator helps runners, walkers, and other athletes determine how much time it takes to cover one kilometer or one mile.

Enter your distance and total running time, and the calculator will automatically calculate your pace per kilometer, pace per mile, and average running speed.

How to Calculate Running Pace

Pace = Total Time ÷ Distance

For example, if you run 5 kilometers in 30 minutes:

30 minutes ÷ 5 km = 6 minutes per kilometer

Your average pace is therefore approximately 6:00 min/km.

How to Use This Pace Calculator

  1. Enter your running or walking distance.
  2. Select kilometers or miles.
  3. Enter your total time in hours, minutes, and seconds.
  4. Click Calculate Pace.
  5. View your pace and average speed.

Why Calculate Your Running Pace?

Knowing your pace can help you understand your running performance, plan workouts, compare different runs, and estimate the time needed to complete a particular distance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is running pace?

Running pace is the amount of time required to cover a specific distance, commonly expressed as minutes per kilometer or minutes per mile.

What is the difference between pace and speed?

Pace measures time per distance, such as minutes per kilometer. Speed measures distance per unit of time, such as kilometers per hour.

Can I use this calculator for walking?

Yes. The calculator can be used for running, walking, jogging, cycling, or any activity where you know the distance and total time.

What is a 5K pace?

A 5K pace is the average amount of time it takes to cover one kilometer during a 5-kilometer run. For example, completing a 5K in 30 minutes gives a pace of 6:00 minutes per kilometer.
