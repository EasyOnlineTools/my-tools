What Is an Exponent?

An exponent is a number that tells you how many times a base is used as a factor in repeated multiplication. In the expression an, the number a is the base and n is the exponent.

an = a × a × ... × a

For example, 23 means 2 × 2 × 2, which equals 8. Exponents are also called powers or indices.

Basic Exponent Rules and Laws

These rules help simplify expressions and solve problems involving powers. They apply under the usual mathematical conditions shown in each rule.

1. Multiplication Rule

When multiplying powers with the same non-zero base, add the exponents.

am × an = am+n

Example: 2² × 2³ = 2⁵ = 32

2. Division Rule

When dividing powers with the same non-zero base, subtract the exponents.

am ÷ an = am-n

Example: 2⁵ ÷ 2² = 2³ = 8

3. Power of a Power

When a power is raised to another power, multiply the exponents.

(am)n = am×n

Example: (2²)³ = 2⁶ = 64

4. Zero Exponent

Any non-zero number raised to the power of zero equals 1.

a0 = 1, when a ≠ 0

Example: 10⁰ = 1

5. Negative Exponent

A negative exponent means taking the reciprocal of the corresponding positive power.

a-n = 1 ÷ an, when a ≠ 0

Example: 2⁻³ = 1 ÷ 2³ = 1/8 = 0.125

6. Exponent of One

A number raised to the power of one remains unchanged.

a1 = a

Example: 7¹ = 7

7. Fractional Exponent

A fractional exponent can represent a root of a number.

a1/2 = √a

Example: 9⁰·⁵ = √9 = 3

Positive and Negative Bases

A negative base raised to a positive whole-number exponent can produce either a positive or negative result. An even exponent produces a positive result, while an odd exponent produces a negative result.

(−2)² = 4   |   (−2)³ = −8

Fractional Powers of Negative Numbers

Some fractional powers of negative numbers do not have real-number answers. For example, the square root of −9 is not a real number. This calculator reports an error when a calculation requires a complex or imaginary result.

What Is Euler's Number (e)?

Euler's number is an important mathematical constant approximately equal to 2.718281828. It is used in exponential growth, compound interest, science, and many areas of mathematics. Select the "Use e as the base" option to calculate powers of e.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an exponent tell us?

An exponent tells us the power to which a base is raised. For a positive whole-number exponent, it represents how many times the base is multiplied by itself.

Can this calculator find a missing base or exponent?

Yes. Enter any two known values in the equation and leave the third field empty. The calculator will try to find the missing value.

How do I calculate a negative exponent?

A negative exponent means taking the reciprocal of the positive power. For example, 2⁻³ = 1/8 = 0.125.

What happens when the exponent is zero?

Any non-zero base raised to the power of zero equals 1. The expression 0⁰ is treated as undefined by this calculator.

Can I use decimal exponents?

Yes. Decimal exponents can represent roots and other fractional powers. For example, 9⁰·⁵ = 3.

Can I calculate powers of negative numbers?

Yes, when the result is a real number. Some fractional powers of negative bases require imaginary numbers and are not calculated by this real-number calculator.
