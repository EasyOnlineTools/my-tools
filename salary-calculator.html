What Is a Salary Calculator?

A Salary Calculator helps convert income between annual, monthly, weekly, daily, and hourly salary amounts. It can be useful when comparing job offers, reviewing compensation, or converting an hourly wage into an estimated annual salary.

How the Salary Calculator Works

Enter your salary amount and select the period that applies to that amount. Then enter your typical work hours per week and the number of working weeks per year. The calculator converts the salary into equivalent amounts for different pay periods.

Salary Conversion Formulas

Annual Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours Per Week × Working Weeks Per Year
Monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 12
Weekly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ Working Weeks Per Year
Daily Salary = Weekly Salary ÷ 5
Hourly Salary = Weekly Salary ÷ Hours Per Week

Example

If your annual salary is $60,000 and you work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks per year, the calculator estimates:

Annual = $60,000
Monthly = $5,000.00
Weekly = $1,153.85
Daily = $230.77
Hourly = $28.85

Choosing Working Weeks Per Year

The default setting is 52 working weeks per year. If you receive unpaid leave or normally work fewer weeks, you can adjust this value to better reflect your work schedule.

Important: This calculator provides gross salary estimates. It does not account for income taxes, social security, benefits, bonuses, overtime, commissions, deductions, unpaid leave, or other compensation adjustments. Actual take-home pay may be different.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this salary calculator based on gross income?

Yes. The calculator works with gross salary before taxes and other deductions.

How many working weeks should I enter?

Enter the number of weeks you normally work during the year. The default is 52 weeks. If you have unpaid leave, you can reduce the number to reflect your actual working schedule.

Can I convert hourly pay to annual salary?

Yes. Select Hourly as the salary period and enter your hourly rate, weekly hours, and working weeks per year.

Can I convert an annual salary to hourly pay?

Yes. Select Annual, enter your annual salary, and provide your typical weekly working hours and working weeks per year.

Does the calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator provides gross salary conversions and does not calculate take-home pay or income taxes.

How is daily salary calculated?

Daily salary is estimated by dividing weekly salary by 5, assuming a five-day working week.
