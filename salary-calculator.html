What Is a Salary Calculator?

A Salary Calculator helps convert income between annual, monthly, weekly, daily, and hourly salary amounts. It can be useful when comparing job offers, understanding compensation, or converting an hourly wage into an estimated annual salary.

How the Salary Calculator Works

Enter your salary amount, select the period that applies to that amount, and enter your typical working hours and weeks per year. The calculator converts the amount into equivalent salary periods.

Salary Conversion Formulas

Annual Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours Per Week × Working Weeks Per Year
Monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 12
Weekly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ Working Weeks Per Year
Daily Salary = Weekly Salary ÷ 5
Hourly Salary = Weekly Salary ÷ Hours Per Week

Example

If your annual salary is $60,000 and you work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks per year, the calculator estimates your equivalent monthly, weekly, daily, and hourly amounts.

Important Note

The results are gross salary estimates and do not account for income tax, social security, benefits, bonuses, overtime, deductions, unpaid leave, or other compensation adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this salary calculator based on gross income?

Yes. The calculator works with gross salary before taxes and other deductions.

How many working weeks should I enter?

Use the number of weeks you normally work during the year. The default is 52 weeks.

Can I convert hourly pay to annual salary?

Yes. Select Hourly as the salary period and enter your hourly rate, weekly hours, and working weeks per year.

Does the calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator provides gross salary conversions and does not calculate take-home pay.
