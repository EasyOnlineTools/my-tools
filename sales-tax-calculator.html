What Is a Sales Tax Calculator?

A Sales Tax Calculator is an online tool that helps you determine how much sales tax will be added to the price of a product or service. It also calculates the final amount after the tax is added.

Instead of calculating the percentage manually, enter the original price and tax rate and the calculator will instantly show the sales tax and total price.

How to Calculate Sales Tax

To calculate sales tax, multiply the original price by the sales tax rate divided by 100.

For example, if an item costs $100 and the sales tax rate is 8%, the sales tax is $8 and the final price including tax is $108.

Sales Tax Formula

Sales Tax = Price × Tax Rate ÷ 100

Total Price = Price + Sales Tax

Example

Suppose a product costs $250 and the sales tax rate is 7.5%.

Sales Tax = $250 × 7.5 ÷ 100 = $18.75

Total Price = $250 + $18.75 = $268.75

Why Use a Sales Tax Calculator?

A sales tax calculator provides a quick way to estimate the tax before making a purchase. It can be useful for shoppers, businesses, students, budgeting, and everyday percentage calculations.

The calculator works directly in your browser and does not require an account or software installation.

Important: Sales tax rates and tax rules vary by location and may change over time. This calculator provides a mathematical estimate based on the tax rate you enter. For actual tax obligations, verify the applicable rate and rules for your location.

Sales Tax Calculator FAQs

How is sales tax calculated?

Sales tax is calculated by multiplying the original price by the sales tax rate divided by 100.

Does the calculator include sales tax in the final price?

Yes. The calculator shows the original price, sales tax amount, and total price after adding the tax.

Can I enter decimal tax rates?

Yes. You can enter decimal tax rates such as 7.5%, 8.25%, or another valid percentage.

Can I calculate sales tax for a zero-price item?

Yes. A price of zero is valid, and the calculated sales tax and total price will also be zero.

Does this calculator determine the tax rate for my location?

No. You must enter the applicable sales tax rate yourself. Tax rates and rules vary by location.
