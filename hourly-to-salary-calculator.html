What Is an Hourly to Salary Calculator?

An Hourly to Salary Calculator converts an hourly wage into estimated weekly, monthly, and annual earnings. It can help compare hourly pay with salaried compensation.

How to Convert Hourly Pay to Annual Salary

Multiply your hourly pay by the number of hours you work each week and then multiply that amount by the number of working weeks in a year.

Hourly to Salary Formula

Weekly Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours Per Week
Annual Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours Per Week × Working Weeks Per Year
Monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 12

Example

If you earn $25 per hour, work 40 hours per week, and work 52 weeks per year:

Annual Salary = $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000

Your estimated annual gross salary would be $52,000 before taxes and other deductions.

Important Note

This calculator provides gross income estimates. It does not account for taxes, overtime, bonuses, unpaid leave, benefits, deductions, or other compensation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate annual salary from hourly pay?

Multiply your hourly rate by your weekly working hours and then multiply by the number of working weeks per year.

What is $25 an hour annually?

At 40 hours per week and 52 working weeks per year, $25 per hour equals an estimated $52,000 per year.

Does this calculator calculate take-home pay?

No. It calculates gross income before taxes and other deductions.

Can I account for unpaid weeks?

Yes. Enter the approximate number of weeks you actually work during the year instead of 52.
