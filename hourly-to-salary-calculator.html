What Is an Hourly to Salary Calculator?

An Hourly to Salary Calculator converts an hourly wage into estimated weekly, monthly, and annual gross earnings. It can help you compare hourly pay with salaried compensation and understand how your hourly rate translates into yearly income.

How to Convert Hourly Pay to Annual Salary

To estimate annual salary from an hourly wage, multiply the hourly pay rate by the number of hours worked each week and then multiply the result by the number of working weeks per year.

Hourly to Salary Formulas

Weekly Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours Per Week
Annual Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours Per Week × Working Weeks Per Year
Monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 12

Example

If you earn $25 per hour, work 40 hours per week, and work 52 weeks per year:

Annual Salary = $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000

Your estimated annual gross salary would be $52,000 before taxes and other deductions.

Understanding the Salary Estimate

The annual estimate depends on your actual working schedule. If you work fewer than 52 weeks because of unpaid leave or other time away from work, enter the approximate number of weeks you actually work during the year.

Monthly salary is calculated by dividing estimated annual salary by 12. This provides an average monthly amount rather than the exact amount of every individual pay period.

Important Note

This calculator provides gross income estimates. It does not account for income taxes, overtime, bonuses, unpaid leave, benefits, deductions, commissions, or other compensation adjustments. Actual take-home pay may be different.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate annual salary from hourly pay?

Multiply your hourly rate by your weekly working hours and then multiply the result by the number of working weeks per year.

What is $25 an hour annually?

At 40 hours per week and 52 working weeks per year, $25 per hour equals an estimated $52,000 per year.

Does this calculator calculate take-home pay?

No. It calculates gross income before taxes and other deductions.

Can I account for unpaid weeks?

Yes. Enter the approximate number of weeks you actually work during the year instead of 52.

What does the monthly salary result mean?

The monthly result is an average based on your estimated annual salary divided by 12. Actual paychecks may vary depending on your employer's pay schedule.
