What Is a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator helps you estimate how much you may need to pay each month for a loan. It can also show the estimated total interest and total amount paid over the loan term.

How to Use the Loan Calculator

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan term. Then click the Calculate Loan button to see your estimated monthly payment and other results.

Loan Payment Formula

For a standard fixed-rate loan, the monthly payment is calculated using the following formula:

M = P × [r(1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Where M is the monthly payment, P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Important Note

This calculator provides an estimate for informational purposes. Actual loan payments may differ because of fees, taxes, insurance, lender requirements and other charges.
