What Is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the proportion of your total body weight that consists of fat. It is different from body weight alone because two people with the same weight can have different amounts of body fat and lean mass.

This calculator provides an estimate using the U.S. Navy body circumference method. It uses measurements such as height, neck, waist, and for women, hip circumference.

Important: This calculator provides an estimate and is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional body-composition testing.

How to Measure for the Calculator

Height

Measure your height without shoes. Enter the measurement in inches.

Neck

Measure around your neck at approximately the level just below the larynx. Keep the tape level without compressing the skin.

Waist

Measure around your waist according to the measurement location used by the U.S. Navy method. Keep the tape level and avoid pulling it excessively tight.

Hip

For the female calculation, measure around the widest part of the hips.

Weight

Enter your current body weight in pounds.

Body Fat Calculation Formula

The U.S. Navy method estimates body fat from body measurements. The formulas used by this calculator are:

Men

Body Fat % = 86.010 × log₁₀(Waist − Neck) − 70.041 × log₁₀(Height) + 36.76

Women

Body Fat % = 163.205 × log₁₀(Waist + Hip − Neck) − 97.684 × log₁₀(Height) − 78.387

After estimating body fat percentage, the calculator estimates fat mass and lean body mass from your total weight.

Understanding the Results

The calculator provides three main measurements:

  • Estimated Body Fat: the estimated percentage of your body weight that is fat.
  • Body Fat Mass: the estimated weight of body fat.
  • Lean Body Mass: your estimated body weight excluding body fat.
Fat Mass = Body Weight × Body Fat %
Lean Body Mass = Body Weight − Fat Mass

Frequently Asked Questions

What does body fat percentage mean?

Body fat percentage represents the estimated percentage of your total body weight that is made up of fat.

What method does this calculator use?

This calculator uses the U.S. Navy body circumference method, which estimates body fat using measurements such as height, neck, waist, and hip circumference.

Why does the female calculation require hip measurement?

The U.S. Navy formula for women includes hip circumference as one of the measurements used to estimate body fat.

Is this body fat calculation exact?

No. It is an estimate. Body composition can be measured using other methods, and results can vary depending on measurement technique and individual characteristics.

Can I use this calculator for health decisions?

The result should be treated as an estimate. For medical or fitness decisions, consider discussing your results with a qualified healthcare or fitness professional.
