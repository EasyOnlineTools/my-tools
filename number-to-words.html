Number to Words Converter

Convert numbers into written English words instantly. Enter a whole number, view the result and copy the words for your documents, assignments or everyday use.

Enter a number from 0 to 999,999,999,999. Commas are optional. Decimal numbers and negative numbers are not supported.

Number in Words

What Is a Number to Words Converter?

A Number to Words Converter changes a numerical value into its written form using English number names. For example, the number 125 becomes "One Hundred Twenty-Five".

This tool is useful when preparing invoices, receipts, applications, educational assignments and other documents where writing a number in words is helpful.

Examples of Numbers in Words

25 = Twenty-Five
125 = One Hundred Twenty-Five
1,250 = One Thousand Two Hundred Fifty
25,000 = Twenty-Five Thousand
1,000,000 = One Million
1,000,000,000 = One Billion

How to Use the Number to Words Converter

  1. Enter a whole number in the input field.
  2. Use commas if you wish; they are optional.
  3. Click the Convert to Words button.
  4. Read the English words displayed in the result area.
  5. Click Copy Words to copy the result.

Supported Number Range

This converter supports whole numbers from zero up to 999,999,999,999 (999 billion, 999 million, 999 thousand and 999). It uses exact integer processing to avoid rounding errors when handling large numbers.

How Does Number-to-Words Conversion Work?

A large number is divided into groups of three digits, starting from the right. These groups represent hundreds, thousands, millions and billions. Each group is converted into words and combined to create the complete result.

For example, 1,250 is divided into 1 thousand and 250. These groups become "One Thousand" and "Two Hundred Fifty", producing "One Thousand Two Hundred Fifty".

Which Number Format Does This Tool Use?

The converter uses the international English number scale: Thousand, Million and Billion. It follows the standard American-style wording without inserting "and" between hundreds and tens. For example, 125 is written as "One Hundred Twenty-Five".

The tool converts whole numbers only. It does not convert decimal values, fractions, negative numbers or currency amounts into words.

Common Uses of Numbers Written in Words

  • Writing amounts and figures in documents.
  • Preparing invoices, receipts and forms.
  • Learning number names in school.
  • Checking the written form of large numbers.
  • Preparing general business and educational documents.

Always review the result before using it in important financial, official or legal documents. Requirements for wording may differ between institutions and countries.

Yes. Click the Copy Words button below the result to copy the English text to your clipboard.

No. It converts plain whole numbers into English words. It does not add currency names or convert decimal amounts into dollars, pounds or other currencies.
