About This PDF to Word Converter

Easy Online Tool provides a simple way to extract selectable text from PDF documents and create editable Word files. You do not need to install additional software or create an account.

The converter works inside your web browser. Your selected PDF is processed on your device rather than uploaded to our website.

This tool focuses on text extraction. Complex page layouts, tables, columns, pictures, and original PDF formatting may not be reproduced exactly. Image-only scanned documents are not supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this PDF to Word converter free?

Yes. You can use this converter free of charge without registration.

2. What type of Word file will I receive?

The tool creates a DOCX file that can be opened and edited in Microsoft Word and other compatible word processors.

3. Will the original formatting remain the same?

The tool extracts text and organizes it into a Word document. It does not guarantee exact reproduction of the original PDF layout, tables, images, or styling.

4. Can I convert a scanned PDF?

No. This version requires selectable text in the PDF. Image-only scanned documents are not supported.

5. Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is processed locally in your browser. The conversion does not upload your document to our website.

6. What is the maximum file size?

You can select a PDF file up to 20 MB. Very large or complex documents may take longer to process.
