Compression Summary

How to Compress Images Online

  1. Click Choose Images or drag and drop your images into the upload area.
  2. Select your preferred output format.
  3. Choose a compression preset or adjust the quality slider.
  4. Click Compress Images to process your selected files.
  5. Compare the original and compressed images.
  6. Download individual images or download all results as a ZIP file.

Why Compress Images?

Large image files take longer to upload, share and load on websites. Image compression can reduce file size, helping improve website loading speed and making images easier to store and share.

Supported Image Formats

  • JPG/JPEG: Suitable for photographs and everyday images. Adjustable quality is available.
  • PNG: Useful for graphics and transparent images. Browser PNG export does not provide a standard quality slider.
  • WebP: A modern image format that supports efficient lossy compression and transparency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. You can use this image compressor for free without creating an account.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing takes place in your browser. Your selected images are not sent to our server.

Can I compress multiple images?

Yes. You can select multiple images, add more images and process them together.

Can I control image quality?

Yes. You can adjust the quality for JPG and WebP output. PNG compression uses a different browser encoding method.

Will compression always reduce file size?

No. Some images may remain the same size or become larger, especially when converting formats or using high quality settings.

Can I download all compressed images together?

Yes. Use the Download All option to download the processed images in one ZIP file.

Does this tool support animated GIFs?

No. This version supports JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images. Animated GIFs are not supported.
