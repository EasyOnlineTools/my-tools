Temperature Conversion Formulas

Fahrenheit = (Celsius × 9/5) + 32
Celsius = (Fahrenheit − 32) × 5/9
Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15
Celsius = Kelvin − 273.15

Common Temperature Conversions

  • 0°C = 32°F = 273.15 K
  • 10°C = 50°F = 283.15 K
  • 20°C = 68°F = 293.15 K
  • 25°C = 77°F = 298.15 K
  • 30°C = 86°F = 303.15 K
  • 37°C = 98.6°F = 310.15 K
  • 100°C = 212°F = 373.15 K

About Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin

Celsius is commonly used around the world for everyday temperature measurements. Fahrenheit is primarily used in the United States and a few other locations.

Kelvin is the SI base unit for thermodynamic temperature and is widely used in science and engineering.

The Kelvin scale begins at absolute zero, which is 0 K or approximately −273.15°C.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 0°C in Fahrenheit?

0°C is equal to 32°F.

What is 100°C in Fahrenheit?

100°C is equal to 212°F.

What is room temperature in Celsius?

Typical indoor room temperature is often around 20°C to 25°C, although comfortable temperatures vary.

Can Celsius be converted to Kelvin?

Yes. Add 273.15 to the Celsius temperature to obtain the equivalent temperature in Kelvin.
