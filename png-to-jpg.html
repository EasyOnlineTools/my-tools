Why Convert PNG to JPG?

JPG is widely used for photographs and online images. It often produces smaller files than PNG, which can help save storage space and make images easier to share or upload.

What Happens to Transparent Areas?

JPG does not support transparency. When a PNG with transparent areas is converted to JPG, those areas must be filled with a solid background color. You can select a background color before conversion.

PNG vs JPG: What's the Difference?

  • PNG: Supports transparency and lossless image storage.
  • JPG: Uses lossy compression and is commonly used for photographs.
  • File size: JPG files are often smaller, but the result depends on the image and quality setting.
