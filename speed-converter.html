About Speed Units

Kilometers per hour (km/h) is commonly used for vehicle speeds in many countries around the world.

Miles per hour (mph) is commonly used for road speeds in the United States and the United Kingdom.

Meters per second (m/s) is an SI unit commonly used in science and engineering. Knots are commonly used in aviation and maritime navigation.

Mach expresses speed relative to the speed of sound. For this calculator, Mach 1 is approximated as 343 meters per second under standard reference conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many mph is 100 km/h?

100 km/h is approximately 62.1371 mph.

How many km/h is 60 mph?

60 mph is approximately 96.5606 km/h.

How fast is one knot?

One knot is equal to 1.852 kilometers per hour.

What is Mach 1?

Mach 1 represents approximately the speed of sound. Its exact speed varies with conditions such as temperature and altitude. This calculator uses 343 m/s as a standard approximation.
