What Is an Average?

An average is a single value used to represent a group of numbers. The arithmetic mean is the most common type of average. It is calculated by adding all the values and dividing the total by the number of values.

For example, the average of 10, 20 and 30 is 20 because their total is 60 and there are three numbers.

Average Formula

Average = Sum of All Values ÷ Number of Values

Suppose you have the numbers 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50.

Their sum is 150 and the total number of values is 5.

Average = 150 ÷ 5 = 30

Understanding the Results

Mean (Arithmetic Average)

The mean is the sum of all numbers divided by their count. It is useful for finding the typical value of a numerical data set.

Median

The median is the middle value after the numbers are arranged from smallest to largest. If there is an even number of values, the median is the average of the two middle values.

Minimum and Maximum

The minimum is the smallest number in the data set. The maximum is the largest number.

Range

The range measures the spread between the largest and smallest values. It is calculated by subtracting the minimum from the maximum.

Range = Maximum − Minimum

Examples of Average Calculations

Example 1: Average of 5, 10 and 15

Sum = 5 + 10 + 15 = 30. Count = 3. Average = 30 ÷ 3 = 10.

Example 2: Average of 12, 18, 20 and 30

Sum = 80. Count = 4. Average = 80 ÷ 4 = 20.

Example 3: Average of Negative and Positive Numbers

Consider -10, 0 and 20. Their sum is 10 and their count is 3. The average is 10 ÷ 3, or approximately 3.333333.

Mean vs Median: What Is the Difference?

The mean uses every number in the calculation, so unusually high or low values can affect it. The median is the middle value and is less affected by extreme values.

For example, in the data set 10, 12, 13, 15 and 100, the mean is 30 while the median is 13. Both describe the data in different ways.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate an average?

Add all the numbers together and divide their sum by the total number of values.

Is average the same as mean?

In common everyday use, average usually refers to the arithmetic mean. There are other types of averages, including the median and mode.

Can I enter negative numbers?

Yes. This calculator supports negative numbers, positive numbers and decimal values.

Can I calculate the average of many numbers?

Yes. You can enter up to 10,000 numbers separated by commas, spaces or new lines.

What happens if I enter invalid text?

The calculator will show an error instead of silently ignoring invalid entries.

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean is the sum divided by the number of values. The median is the middle value after sorting the numbers.

What is the range of a data set?

The range is the difference between the largest and smallest numbers in a data set.
