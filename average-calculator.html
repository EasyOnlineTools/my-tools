What is an Average?

The average, also called the arithmetic mean, is calculated by adding all numbers together and dividing the total by the number of values.

For example, the average of 10, 20 and 30 is 20.

How to Use the Average Calculator

Enter your numbers in the box. You can separate numbers with commas, spaces, or new lines. Click the Calculate Average button to instantly see the result.
