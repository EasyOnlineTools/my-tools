Length Conversion

The Length Converter helps you convert measurements between common metric and imperial units. You can convert meters, kilometers, centimeters, millimeters, miles, yards, feet, inches and nautical miles.

This free online converter is useful for everyday measurements, school work, construction, engineering, travel, fitness and other situations where different length units need to be converted.

Common Length Conversions

Some commonly used length conversions include:

  • 1 kilometer = 1,000 meters
  • 1 meter = 100 centimeters
  • 1 meter = 1,000 millimeters
  • 1 mile = 5,280 feet
  • 1 yard = 3 feet
  • 1 foot = 12 inches
