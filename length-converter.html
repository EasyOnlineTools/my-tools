What Is a Length Converter?

A length converter changes a measurement from one unit of distance to another. For example, you can convert kilometers to miles, meters to feet, inches to centimeters, or nautical miles to kilometers without calculating the conversion manually.

This free tool supports common metric and imperial measurements and can help with school assignments, travel, DIY projects, construction planning, and everyday measurements.

The converter uses standard unit conversion factors. Results may be displayed in scientific notation when a value is extremely large or small.

Common Length Conversion Facts

  • 1 kilometer = 1,000 meters
  • 1 meter = 100 centimeters
  • 1 meter = 1,000 millimeters
  • 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters
  • 1 foot = 12 inches
  • 1 yard = 3 feet
  • 1 mile = 5,280 feet
  • 1 nautical mile = 1,852 meters

How to Use the Length Converter

  1. Enter the length you want to convert.
  2. Choose the original measurement unit.
  3. Choose the unit you want to convert to.
  4. Select Convert Length to view the result.
  5. Select Reset to clear the value and result.
