    What Is a Fraction Calculator?

    A fraction calculator is a mathematical tool that helps you perform calculations using fractions. It can add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions, including mixed numbers and fractions with different denominators.

    This calculator also simplifies fractions, converts decimals to fractions and fractions to decimals, and displays the steps used to reach the answer.

    How to Use This Fraction Calculator

    Select the type of calculation you need. For ordinary fraction calculations, enter the numerator and denominator of both fractions. You can also enter a whole number to work with a mixed number.

    Choose addition, subtraction, multiplication or division, then select Calculate Fractions. The simplified answer, mixed number, decimal value and calculation steps will appear below.

    Fraction Formulas

    Adding Fractions

    To add fractions with different denominators, find a common denominator, convert both fractions and add their numerators.

    Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6

    Subtracting Fractions

    Convert the fractions to a common denominator and subtract the second numerator from the first.

    Example: 3/4 − 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2

    Multiplying Fractions

    Multiply the numerators together and multiply the denominators together. Simplify the resulting fraction if possible.

    Example: 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2

    Dividing Fractions

    To divide fractions, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

    Example: 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 2

    What Is a Mixed Number?

    A mixed number contains a whole number and a proper fraction. For example, 2 1/3 means two whole units and one-third of another. To convert a positive mixed number to an improper fraction, multiply the whole number by the denominator and add the numerator. Keep the original denominator.

    Example: 2 1/3 = (2 × 3 + 1)/3 = 7/3

    How to Simplify Fractions

    A fraction is in simplest form when its numerator and denominator have no common factor greater than 1. Divide both by their greatest common divisor (GCD) to simplify the fraction.

    Example: 12/18 = (12 ÷ 6)/(18 ÷ 6) = 2/3

    Converting Decimals and Fractions

    Decimal to Fraction

    A terminating decimal can be written as a fraction by placing its digits over a power of 10, then simplifying.

    Example: 0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4

    Fraction to Decimal

    Divide the numerator by the denominator to convert a fraction into a decimal.

    Example: 3/4 = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75

    Common Uses of Fractions

    Fractions are used in school mathematics, measurements, cooking, construction, proportions, shopping and many everyday calculations. This calculator can help check your work and understand how fraction operations are performed.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I add fractions with different denominators?

    Yes. Enter both fractions and choose addition. The calculator finds a common denominator and simplifies the answer.

    Can I calculate mixed numbers?

    Yes. Enter the whole number, numerator and denominator for each fraction. The whole-number fields are optional.

    How do I simplify a fraction?

    Select Simplify a Fraction, enter its numerator and denominator, and click Simplify Fraction. The calculator divides both values by their greatest common divisor.

    Can I convert a decimal to a fraction?

    Yes. Select Decimal to Fraction, enter a terminating decimal and click Convert to Fraction. The result is simplified.

    Can I convert a fraction to a decimal?

    Yes. Select Fraction to Decimal, enter the numerator and denominator and click Convert to Decimal.

    What happens if I enter a zero denominator?

    A denominator cannot be zero. The calculator displays an error message instead of performing an invalid calculation.

    Does the calculator show working steps?

    Yes. The result includes a step-by-step explanation of the calculation, including the common denominator or reciprocal where applicable.
