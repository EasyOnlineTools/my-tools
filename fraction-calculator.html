What is a Fraction Calculator?

A fraction calculator is an online tool that performs mathematical operations with fractions quickly and accurately.

You can use this calculator to add, subtract, multiply and divide two fractions. The final answer is automatically simplified.

How to Use the Fraction Calculator

Enter the numerator and denominator of the first fraction. Enter the numerator and denominator of the second fraction. Select the mathematical operation and click Calculate.
