What Is a Fraction Calculator?

A Fraction Calculator is an online tool that helps you perform calculations with fractions quickly and accurately. You can use it to add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions.

How to Calculate Fractions

Different operations use different methods. When adding or subtracting fractions, the fractions generally need a common denominator. When multiplying fractions, multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.

When dividing fractions, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

What Is a Simplified Fraction?

A simplified fraction is written in its lowest terms. This means the numerator and denominator have no common factor greater than 1.

Why Use a Fraction Calculator?

Working with fractions manually can sometimes be complicated. A fraction calculator makes it easier to perform common fraction operations and check mathematical results.

Fraction Calculator FAQs

Can a fraction calculator add fractions?

Yes. A fraction calculator can add fractions and provide the resulting fraction.

How do you subtract fractions?

Fractions with different denominators generally need to be converted to equivalent fractions with a common denominator before subtraction.

How do you multiply fractions?

Multiply the numerators together and multiply the denominators together. The resulting fraction can then be simplified.

How do you divide fractions?

To divide one fraction by another, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
