Energy Converter

The Energy Converter allows you to convert between commonly used energy units quickly and easily. It supports joules, kilojoules, megajoules, calories, kilocalories, watt-hours, kilowatt-hours, BTU and several other energy measurements.

Common Energy Units

Joule (J): The SI unit used to measure energy.

Kilojoule (kJ): Equal to 1,000 joules and commonly used for food energy and scientific measurements.

Calorie (cal): A traditional unit of energy.

Kilocalorie (kcal): Equal to 1,000 calories and commonly used when describing food energy.

Watt-hour (Wh): A unit of energy commonly used for electrical energy.

Kilowatt-hour (kWh): A widely used unit for measuring electrical energy consumption.

BTU: British Thermal Unit, commonly used in heating, cooling and energy applications.

Energy Conversion Formula

All conversions are performed by first converting the entered value to Joules (J), then converting the Joule value to the selected target unit.
