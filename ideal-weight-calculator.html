What Is an Ideal Weight?

Ideal weight is an estimated healthy body-weight range based on factors such as height and gender. There is no single perfect weight for everyone.

How Is Ideal Weight Calculated?

This calculator uses the Devine formula to provide an estimated ideal body weight based on height and gender.

Devine Formula

For men: 50 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 feet.

For women: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 feet.

Important Note

This calculator provides an estimate for general informational purposes. Healthy weight can vary based on body composition, muscle mass, age, and other factors. It should not be considered medical advice.
