How to Calculate Age: Formula, Examples & Easy Guide

Calculating age may seem simple, but finding an exact age in years, months and days requires comparing two dates carefully. This guide explains how to calculate age from a date of birth using simple methods and practical examples.

Whether you want to calculate your current age, determine someone's age on a particular date, or find the exact difference between two dates, understanding how age calculation works can be useful for everyday, school, work and official purposes.

How Is Age Calculated?

Age is generally calculated by comparing a person's date of birth with the date on which their age is being calculated.

The basic calculation starts with the difference between the current year and the birth year. However, the result must be adjusted if the person's birthday has not yet occurred during the current year.

Basic Age = Current Year − Birth Year

If the birthday has not occurred yet in the current year, subtract one year from the result.

Simple Example of Age Calculation

Suppose a person was born on June 15, 1990, and we want to calculate their age on September 15, 2026.

Current year: 2026

Birth year: 1990

2026 − 1990 = 36

Because June 15 has already passed by September 15, the person is:

36 years old

What If the Birthday Has Not Happened Yet?

Consider another example. Suppose a person was born on December 20, 1990, and we calculate their age on September 15, 2026.

2026 − 1990 = 36

However, December 20 has not yet occurred in 2026.

Therefore, subtract one year:

36 − 1 = 35 years old

This birthday adjustment is important when calculating someone's completed age in years.

How to Calculate Exact Age in Years, Months and Days

Calculating exact age is more detailed than simply subtracting two years. You need to compare the day, month and year of the two dates.

For example, if someone was born on January 10, 2000 and the calculation date is March 25, 2026, their age can be expressed as:

26 years, 2 months and 15 days

A date-based calculator can perform this calculation automatically and handle different month lengths and leap years.

How to Calculate Age From Date of Birth

Follow these steps to calculate someone's age:

  1. Write down the person's date of birth.
  2. Identify the date on which the age should be calculated.
  3. Subtract the birth year from the target year.
  4. Check whether the birthday has already occurred in the target year.
  5. Subtract one year if the birthday has not yet occurred.
  6. For an exact result, compare the months and days as well.

How to Calculate Age on a Specific Date

You do not always need to calculate someone's age as of today. Sometimes you may need to know how old a person was on a particular historical or future date.

The same method can be used:

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Choose the specific target date.
  3. Compare the two dates.
  4. Calculate the difference in years, months and days.

This can be useful for calculating age on an application date, examination date, event date or another specific date.

Age Calculation and Leap Years

Leap years can make manual date calculations more complicated because February can contain 29 days instead of 28.

A leap year generally occurs every four years, with special rules for century years. Because of this, an exact age calculation should account for the actual calendar dates rather than simply assuming every year contains the same number of days.

Tip: When you need an exact age in years, months and days, use a date-based calculator instead of manually counting the days.

Why Manual Age Calculations Can Be Difficult

Basic age calculations are easy, but exact date differences can become complicated because months have different numbers of days.

January has 31 days, February usually has 28 days, and other months have either 30 or 31 days. Leap years also change the number of days in February.

This is why an automated calculator can be useful when you need an exact result.

Common Age Calculation Mistakes

When calculating age manually, these are some common mistakes to avoid:

  • Forgetting to check whether the birthday has already occurred.
  • Ignoring the month and day when calculating exact age.
  • Assuming every year has exactly the same number of days.
  • Forgetting about leap years.
  • Confusing birth year with completed age.
