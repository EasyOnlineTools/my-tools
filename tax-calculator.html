What is a Sales Tax Calculator?

A Sales Tax Calculator helps you quickly determine how much sales tax will be added to the original price of a product or service.

Simply enter the original price and sales tax percentage. The calculator will show the tax amount and the final price including tax.

How to Calculate Sales Tax

Sales Tax = Original Price × (Tax Rate ÷ 100)

Total Price = Original Price + Sales Tax
