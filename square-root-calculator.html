What Is a Square Root?

A square root is a number that, when multiplied by itself, produces the original number.

√N = R

This means that:

R × R = N

For example, the square root of 144 is 12 because:

12 × 12 = 144

Square Root Examples

  • √4 = 2
  • √9 = 3
  • √16 = 4
  • √25 = 5
  • √100 = 10
  • √144 = 12
  • √225 = 15

Square Roots of Non-Perfect Squares

Not every number has a whole-number square root. For example, √10 is approximately 3.16227766.

√10 ≈ 3.16227766

Square Root of Zero

The square root of zero is zero.

√0 = 0
