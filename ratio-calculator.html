      What Is a Ratio?

      A ratio is a mathematical comparison between two or more quantities. It shows how much of one quantity exists compared with another. Ratios are commonly written using a colon, such as 2:3.

      For example, if a group has 10 boys and 15 girls, the ratio of boys to girls is 10:15. This ratio can be simplified to 2:3.

      How to Use the Ratio Calculator

      The first calculator solves a proportion. Enter any three known values in A:B = C:D and leave the missing value blank. The calculator finds the fourth value.

      The Ratio Scaling Calculator takes an existing ratio and multiplies or divides both parts by the same scaling factor. This creates an equivalent ratio.

      Ratio Formula

      A proportion is an equation that states that two ratios are equal:

      A:B = C:D

      Cross multiplication gives:

      A × D = B × C

      This formula can be rearranged to find any one missing value, provided the required divisor is not zero.

      Example: Find a Missing Value

      2:3 = 8:D

      Cross multiply: 2 × D = 3 × 8

      2D = 24

      D = 24 ÷ 2

      Answer: D = 12

      What Is Ratio Scaling?

      Ratio scaling means multiplying or dividing both parts of a ratio by the same positive number. The relationship between the two quantities stays the same.

      Example: Multiply 2:3 by 4

      2 × 4 = 8

      3 × 4 = 12

      New ratio: 8:12

      Equivalent Ratios

      Equivalent ratios describe the same relationship between quantities. For example, 2:3, 4:6, 6:9 and 8:12 are equivalent ratios.

      Common Uses of Ratios

      Ratios are used in cooking, maps, scale drawings, measurements, finance, statistics, recipes and everyday comparisons. They help people understand relationships between quantities.

      Ratio Calculator FAQs

      How do I find a missing value in a ratio?

      Enter the three known values in A:B = C:D and leave the missing field empty. The calculator uses cross multiplication to find the missing value.

      What does A:B = C:D mean?

      It means that the ratio of A to B is equal to the ratio of C to D. This relationship is called a proportion.

      What is cross multiplication?

      Cross multiplication is a method used to solve proportions. For A:B = C:D, multiply A by D and B by C. The two products are equal when the proportion is correct.

      What is a ratio scaling calculator?

      It creates an equivalent ratio by multiplying or dividing both parts of an original ratio by the same positive number.

      Can I use decimal values?

      Yes. Both calculators accept decimal values. Use up to 10 decimal places for accurate calculations.

      Can I enter zero?

      Zero can be used in some ratio calculations, but a proportion cannot be solved when its required divisor is zero. The calculator displays an error if the calculation is undefined.
