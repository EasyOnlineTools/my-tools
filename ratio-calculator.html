What is a Ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities and shows the relationship between them. Ratios are commonly written using a colon, such as 2:3.

For example, if there are 10 apples and 5 oranges, the ratio of apples to oranges is 10:5, which simplifies to 2:1.

How to Simplify a Ratio

To simplify a ratio, divide both numbers by their greatest common factor. For example, a ratio of 10:15 can be simplified to 2:3 because both numbers can be divided by 5.

Ratio Formula

A ratio compares two quantities and is commonly written in the form a:b. The first number represents one quantity and the second number represents the quantity being compared with it.

For example, if a classroom has 20 boys and 30 girls, the ratio of boys to girls is 20:30. Dividing both numbers by 10 gives the simplified ratio of 2:3.

Equivalent Ratios

Equivalent ratios describe the same relationship between quantities. You can create an equivalent ratio by multiplying or dividing both parts by the same non-zero number.

For example, 2:3, 4:6 and 6:9 are equivalent ratios because they all represent the same relationship.

Ratio, Decimal and Percentage

A ratio can also be expressed as a decimal or percentage when comparing the first quantity with the second. This calculator displays the decimal relationship and the first number's percentage of the second number when the second number is not zero.

Common Uses of Ratios

Ratios are commonly used in mathematics, recipes, maps, scale drawings, measurements, statistics, business and everyday comparisons. They help describe how one quantity relates to another.

Ratio Calculator FAQs

What does a ratio such as 2:3 mean?

A ratio of 2:3 means that for every 2 units of the first quantity, there are 3 units of the second quantity.

How do I simplify a ratio?

Divide both numbers by their greatest common factor. For example, 12:18 becomes 2:3 after dividing both numbers by 6.

Can the Ratio Calculator handle zero?

Yes. One number can be zero, but both numbers cannot be zero. The calculator also prevents division by zero when calculating the decimal and percentage relationship.

Can I enter decimal numbers?

The calculator accepts numeric input, including decimal values. For ratios involving decimals, the simplified result may also contain decimal values depending on the numbers entered.
