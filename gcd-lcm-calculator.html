What Are GCD and LCM?

GCD and LCM are important mathematical concepts used with whole numbers, factors and multiples. The GCD is the largest positive integer that divides two numbers without a remainder. The LCM is the smallest positive integer that is divisible by both numbers.

GCD Formula

The Greatest Common Divisor (GCD), also called the Greatest Common Factor (GCF), is the largest number that divides both input numbers exactly.

GCD(a, b) = Greatest common factor of a and b

LCM Formula

The Least Common Multiple is the smallest positive number that is a multiple of both input numbers.

LCM(a, b) = (a × b) ÷ GCD(a, b)

This relationship is used by the calculator to find the LCM after calculating the GCD.

Example: Find the GCD and LCM of 12 and 18

The factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12. The factors of 18 are 1, 2, 3, 6, 9 and 18. Their greatest common factor is 6.

The multiples of 12 include 12, 24, 36 and 48. The multiples of 18 include 18, 36 and 54. Their smallest common multiple is 36.

GCD(12, 18) = 6   |   LCM(12, 18) = 36

How Does the Euclidean Algorithm Work?

The Euclidean algorithm is an efficient method for finding the GCD of two positive integers. Divide the larger number by the smaller number and replace the pair with the smaller number and the remainder. Repeat until the remainder is zero. The last non-zero remainder is the GCD.

For example, to find the GCD of 48 and 18:

  • 48 ÷ 18 = 2 remainder 12
  • 18 ÷ 12 = 1 remainder 6
  • 12 ÷ 6 = 2 remainder 0

Therefore, the GCD of 48 and 18 is 6.

Common Uses of GCD and LCM

  • Finding common denominators when adding or subtracting fractions.
  • Solving mathematical and number theory problems.
  • Working out repeating schedules and events.
  • Dividing quantities into equal groups.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does GCD stand for?

GCD stands for Greatest Common Divisor. It is the largest positive integer that divides two or more numbers without a remainder.

What does LCM stand for?

LCM stands for Least Common Multiple. It is the smallest positive integer that is divisible by two or more numbers.

What is the GCD of 12 and 18?

The GCD of 12 and 18 is 6 because 6 is their greatest common factor.

What is the LCM of 4 and 6?

The LCM of 4 and 6 is 12 because 12 is the smallest positive number divisible by both 4 and 6.
