What Is a Case Converter?

A case converter is an online text formatting tool that changes the capitalization of letters in your text. It can convert text into uppercase, lowercase, title case and other common styles.

This free online case converter is useful for students, teachers, writers, bloggers, office workers and anyone who needs to format text quickly.

Available Text Conversion Options

1. Uppercase

Converts all letters into capital letters. Example: hello world becomes HELLO WORLD.

2. Lowercase

Converts all letters into small letters. Example: HELLO WORLD becomes hello world.

3. Title Case

Capitalizes the first letter of each word while converting the remaining letters to lowercase. Example: welcome to my website becomes Welcome To My Website.

4. Sentence Case

Capitalizes the first letter of each sentence and converts the remaining letters to lowercase.

5. Capitalize Each Word

Capitalizes the first letter of each word while preserving the remaining letters as they are.

6. Inverse Case

Changes uppercase letters to lowercase and lowercase letters to uppercase.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this case converter free?

Yes. This online case converter is free to use and does not require registration.

Can I convert an entire paragraph?

Yes. You can paste a single sentence, multiple paragraphs or a large amount of text into the input box.

Does sentence case work automatically?

Yes. Sentence case uses punctuation to identify sentence beginnings and capitalize their first letters. Abbreviations and unusual punctuation may affect the result.

Can I download converted text?

Yes. Select Download Text to save the converted result as a plain text file.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion JavaScript processes your text locally in your browser. The tool does not submit your text to a server.
