What Is a Calorie Calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates how many calories your body may need each day. It uses information such as age, gender, weight, height, and physical activity.

What Is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is an estimate of the calories your body needs to perform basic functions while at rest.

What Are Maintenance Calories?

Maintenance calories are an estimate of the calories needed to maintain your current weight based on your activity level.

Important Note

These results are estimates and should not be considered medical advice. Individual calorie requirements can vary depending on many factors.
