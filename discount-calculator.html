How Does the Discount Calculator Work?

Our discount calculator uses the original price and discount percentage to determine how much money you save and what the final sale price will be.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Final Price Formula

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If an item costs $100 and has a 20% discount:

Discount = $100 × 20 ÷ 100 = $20

Final Price = $100 − $20 = $80

You save $20 and pay only $80.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a discount calculator?

A discount calculator helps you quickly determine the amount you save on an item and the final price after applying a discount.

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100. For example, a $100 item with a 20% discount saves $20, making the final price $80.

Can I use this calculator for any currency?

Yes. The calculator works with any currency. The dollar symbol shown in the results is simply a default display symbol.

Can I calculate discounts with decimal percentages?

Yes. You can enter decimal percentages such as 12.5%, 15.5%, or 22.75%.

Note: This calculator provides mathematical estimates and does not include additional taxes, shipping charges, fees, or other costs that may apply to a purchase.
