How Does the Discount Calculator Work?

A discount calculator uses the original price and discount percentage to determine the amount saved and the final price after the discount is applied.

Discount Amount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Final Price Formula

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If an item costs $100 and has a 20% discount:

Discount Amount = $100 × 20 ÷ 100 = $20

Final Price = $100 − $20 = $80

You save $20 and pay $80.

Why Use a Discount Calculator?

Discounts are common during sales, promotions, clearance events, and special offers. Calculating the sale price manually can be inconvenient when comparing several products. This calculator provides a quick way to estimate the savings and final price.

Discounts in Everyday Shopping

You can use the calculator for clothing, electronics, groceries, furniture, online purchases, and other products where a percentage discount is applied to the original price.

Discount Calculator FAQs

What is a discount calculator?

A discount calculator helps determine how much money is saved from a percentage discount and what the final sale price will be.

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100. For example, a 20% discount on $100 is $20, leaving a final price of $80.

What is the sale price after a discount?

The sale price is the original price minus the discount amount.

How much is 20% off $100?

A 20% discount on $100 saves $20, so the final price is $80.

Can I use decimal discount percentages?

Yes. You can enter decimal percentages such as 12.5%, 15.5%, or 22.75%.

Can I use this calculator for any currency?

Yes. The mathematical calculation works with any currency. The dollar symbol displayed in the results is only a default display symbol.
