How Does the Discount Calculator Work?

Our discount calculator uses the original price and discount percentage to determine how much money you save and what the final sale price will be.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Final Price Formula

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If an item costs $100 and has a 20% discount:

Discount = $100 × 20 ÷ 100 = $20

Final Price = $100 − $20 = $80

You save $20 and pay only $80.
