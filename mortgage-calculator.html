How Does a Mortgage Calculator Work?

A mortgage calculator estimates the monthly payment required to repay a home loan based on the loan amount, interest rate, and loan term.

The calculation uses the standard mortgage payment formula and assumes regular monthly payments throughout the loan term.

Mortgage Payment Formula

Monthly Payment = P × [r(1+r)n] ÷ [(1+r)n − 1]

P = Loan amount
r = Monthly interest rate
n = Total number of monthly payments

This calculator provides an estimate. Actual mortgage costs may also include taxes, insurance, fees, and other charges.
