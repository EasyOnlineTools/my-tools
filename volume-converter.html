Understanding Volume Measurements

Liters and milliliters

Liters and milliliters are metric units used for measuring liquids and other volumes. One liter contains 1,000 milliliters. Milliliters are useful for smaller amounts, such as beverages, cooking ingredients, and liquid products.

US customary and UK imperial units

Gallons, quarts, pints, cups, and fluid ounces are used in different measurement systems. A US gallon is smaller than a UK imperial gallon, and the same distinction applies to several related units. The calculator keeps these units separate to help prevent incorrect conversions.

Cubic units

Cubic meters, cubic feet, cubic inches, and cubic centimeters measure three-dimensional space. They are often used for containers, rooms, construction, storage, and engineering. One cubic centimeter is equal to one milliliter.
