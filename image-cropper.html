How to Crop an Image Online

  1. Click Choose Image or drag an image into the upload area.
  2. Select an aspect ratio or keep Free Crop.
  3. Drag your mouse or finger over the image to select the area you want to keep.
  4. Click Crop Image to create the cropped preview.
  5. Choose your output format and quality.
  6. Click Download Cropped Image to save your result.

Why Use an Image Cropper?

An image cropper helps remove unwanted areas from photographs and pictures. You can focus attention on the main subject, prepare images for social media, create profile pictures or adjust photos for websites.

What Is an Aspect Ratio?

An aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between an image's width and height. For example, a 1:1 ratio produces a square image, while 16:9 creates a widescreen shape.

Is This Image Cropper Private?

Yes. Image processing happens directly in your browser. Your selected images are not uploaded to our server by this tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this image cropper free?

Yes. You can crop images online for free without creating an account.

2. Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images.

3. Can I crop a square profile picture?

Yes. Select the Square (1:1) aspect ratio and choose the area you want to keep.

4. Can I crop images on my mobile phone?

Yes. The cropper supports touch selection on mobile devices.

5. Can I download my cropped image as PNG or JPG?

Yes. You can select PNG, JPG or WebP as the output format.

6. Does cropping reduce image quality?

Cropping removes pixels outside the selected area. PNG can preserve the cropped pixels without lossy compression, while JPG and WebP quality settings may affect the output.

7. What is the maximum upload size?

This tool accepts images up to 10 MB.
