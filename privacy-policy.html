Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Easy Online Tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information related to your use of our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit Easy Online Tool, how that information may be used, and how cookies, analytics services, advertising services and third-party services may operate on the website.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Easy Online Tool does not require visitors to create an account, provide a password, or submit personal information in order to use the calculators and tools.

Some information may nevertheless be collected automatically by the website or by third-party services used on the website. This may include information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate location, referring pages, date and time of visits, and general usage information.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, message, questions, feedback or suggestions. This information is used only for responding to your communication and providing appropriate assistance.

3. Calculator and Tool Data

Many Easy Online Tool calculators are designed to perform calculations directly in your web browser. The information you enter into a calculator is generally used to produce the requested result and is not intentionally stored by Easy Online Tool on a personal account or user profile.

However, information submitted to a calculator may be processed by your browser or device, and normal website technologies or third-party services may collect technical usage information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Easy Online Tool and third-party service providers may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, local storage or similar technologies for purposes such as website functionality, analytics, advertising, security, performance measurement and understanding general website usage.

Cookies are small files or identifiers that may be stored on your browser or device. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some website features may not function as intended if certain technologies are disabled.

5. Advertising

Easy Online Tool may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers, including Google, if advertising services are enabled on the website.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites, subject to the applicable settings, policies and laws.

Google may use advertising cookies to help serve relevant advertisements, measure advertising performance and limit repeated advertisements. Google explains that users can manage personalized advertising through its advertising settings.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use partners' sites or apps, please visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

Users may also manage Google's advertising personalization settings through: Google Ads Settings .

Advertising services and their use of cookies may vary depending on the advertising providers enabled on the website and the visitor's location, device and privacy choices.

6. Analytics and Website Performance

We may use analytics or similar services to understand general website traffic, usage patterns, technical performance and how visitors interact with our tools.

Analytics services may collect information such as pages viewed, browser and device information, approximate geographic information, referring sources and general interaction data.

These services are used to help us improve the website and its tools. Any third-party analytics service used on the website is subject to its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

Easy Online Tool may use third-party services for functions such as website analytics, advertising, hosting, security, performance monitoring or other website-related purposes.
