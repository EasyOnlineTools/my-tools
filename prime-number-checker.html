What is a Prime Number?

A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 that has exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself.

Examples of prime numbers include 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19 and 23.

What is a Composite Number?

A composite number is a whole number greater than 1 that has more than two factors. For example, 12 has factors 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12.

How to Use the Prime Number Checker

Enter a whole number and click "Check Number". The calculator will tell you whether the number is prime or not.
