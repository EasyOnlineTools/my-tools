Why Remove Extra Spaces?

Extra spaces and unnecessary blank lines can make documents difficult to read and format. Cleaning text helps improve readability and makes content easier to reuse in emails, documents, reports, and web pages.

This tool processes your text locally in your browser. Your text is not uploaded to a server by this tool.

Can I remove extra blank lines?

Yes. Select the extra blank lines option to reduce repeated blank lines while keeping normal paragraph breaks.

Can I remove all line breaks?

Yes. Select the option to convert all whitespace into single spaces. This combines your text into one continuous line.

Is my text uploaded or stored?

No. Text cleaning happens directly in your browser. This page does not send your text to a server.
