Area Units Explained

Square meters are the standard metric unit commonly used to measure the area of rooms, buildings and land.

Square feet are widely used for homes, buildings and property measurements, particularly in the United States and several other countries.

Acres are commonly used for measuring larger areas of land, while hectares are widely used internationally for agricultural and land measurements.
