Understanding Area Units

Square meters

The square meter is the standard SI unit of area. It is commonly used for rooms, houses, offices, and land measurements in metric countries.

Square feet and square yards

Square feet are commonly used for houses, apartments, and property measurements in countries such as the United States. Square yards are often used for land, flooring, and fabric-related measurements.

Acres and hectares

Acres are frequently used to measure land and property. A hectare equals 10,000 square meters and is commonly used for agriculture, farms, and large land areas.

Square kilometers and square miles

These larger units are useful for measuring cities, regions, forests, and other extensive areas of land.
