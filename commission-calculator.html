What Is a Commission Calculator?

A Commission Calculator helps salespeople, agents, employees, freelancers, and businesses calculate commission earnings based on a sales amount and commission percentage.

Enter the total sales amount and your commission rate to find out how much commission you will earn and the combined amount after adding the commission.

Commission Formula

Commission = Sales Amount × (Commission Rate ÷ 100)
Total Earnings = Sales Amount + Commission

Commission Calculator Example

If your sales amount is $10,000 and your commission rate is 5%:

Commission = $10,000 × (5 ÷ 100) = $500
Total Earnings = $10,000 + $500 = $10,500

How to Use the Commission Calculator

  1. Enter the total sales amount.
  2. Enter the commission rate as a percentage.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. Review the commission earned and total earnings.

Understanding Commission Rates

Commission rates vary depending on the type of sales, employment agreement, business arrangement, product, industry, or contract. Always use the commission rate that applies to your specific situation.

Important: This calculator assumes that commission is calculated as a simple percentage of the sales amount. Some commission plans may include thresholds, bonuses, deductions, tiered rates, refunds, or other conditions that are not included in this calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is commission calculated?

Commission is calculated by multiplying the sales amount by the commission rate divided by 100.

What is a 10% commission on $5,000?

A 10% commission on $5,000 is $500.

Can I use this calculator for different commission rates?

Yes. You can enter different commission percentages and calculate the corresponding commission.

Does this calculator support a commission rate above 100%?

Yes. The calculator accepts rates above 100% when entered. However, unusually high commission rates should be checked against the applicable sales agreement.
