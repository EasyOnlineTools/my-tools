What Is a Commission Calculator?

A Commission Calculator helps salespeople, agents, employees, freelancers, and businesses calculate commission earnings based on a sales amount and commission percentage.

Enter the total sales amount and your commission rate to find out how much commission you will earn and your total amount after adding the commission.

Commission Formula

Commission = Sales Amount × (Commission Rate ÷ 100)
Total Earnings = Sales Amount + Commission

Example

If your sales amount is $10,000 and your commission rate is 5%:

Commission = $10,000 × 5% = $500

Total Earnings = $10,000 + $500 = $10,500

Frequently Asked Questions

How is commission calculated?

Commission is calculated by multiplying the sales amount by the commission rate divided by 100.

What is a 10% commission on $5,000?

A 10% commission on $5,000 is $500.

Can I use this calculator for different commission rates?

Yes. You can enter any valid commission percentage and calculate the corresponding commission.
