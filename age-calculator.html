What Is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is a free online tool that calculates a person's exact age based on their date of birth and a selected date. It can show age in years, months and days.

How Does an Age Calculator Work?

Enter your date of birth and the date you want to calculate the age on. The calculator compares the two dates and determines the difference in years, months and days.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

Calculating an exact age manually can be difficult because months have different numbers of days and leap years can affect date calculations. An online Age Calculator makes the calculation quick and convenient.

When Can an Age Calculator Be Useful?

An Age Calculator can be useful for checking birthdays, calculating age for forms and applications, determining age on a particular date, or simply finding out exactly how old someone is.

Age Calculator FAQs

How is age calculated?

Age is calculated by finding the difference between a person's date of birth and the selected date.

Can an Age Calculator show months and days?
