What Is a Salary to Hourly Calculator?

A Salary to Hourly Calculator converts a fixed salary into an estimated hourly wage based on your working hours and the number of weeks you work each year. It can help you understand the hourly equivalent of a salaried job.

How to Convert Salary to Hourly Pay

First, determine the annual salary. If you enter a monthly or weekly salary, the calculator converts it to an annual amount. The annual salary is then divided by the total number of hours worked during the year.

Salary to Hourly Formulas

Hourly Pay = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours Per Week × Working Weeks Per Year)
Weekly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ Working Weeks Per Year
Monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 12

Example

If your annual salary is $60,000, you work 40 hours per week, and work 52 weeks per year:

Hourly Pay = $60,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = $28.85

Your estimated hourly equivalent would be approximately $28.85 per hour.

Understanding the Calculation

Your hourly equivalent depends on both your salary and your working schedule. Working fewer hours each week results in a higher hourly equivalent for the same annual salary, while working more hours results in a lower hourly equivalent.

The calculator uses your selected number of working weeks rather than automatically assuming that you work every week of the year. This allows you to account for unpaid time away from work.

Important Note

This calculator provides a gross salary estimate. It does not account for taxes, bonuses, overtime, benefits, unpaid leave, paid vacation, deductions, or other compensation. Actual take-home pay may be different.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert annual salary to hourly pay?

Divide your annual salary by the total number of hours you work in a year. Total annual working hours are calculated by multiplying weekly working hours by working weeks per year.

What is $60,000 a year per hour?

Assuming 40 hours per week and 52 working weeks per year, $60,000 per year is approximately $28.85 per hour.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator converts gross salary and does not calculate after-tax income.

Can I account for unpaid weeks?

Yes. Enter the number of weeks you actually expect to work during the year instead of 52.

What happens if I change my weekly working hours?

Changing your weekly hours changes the estimated hourly equivalent. For example, the same annual salary divided by fewer working hours produces a higher hourly equivalent.
