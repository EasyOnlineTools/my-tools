How Much Water Should You Drink?

Water requirements vary from person to person. Body size, physical activity, climate, diet, and other factors can affect how much fluid a person needs.

How This Calculator Works

This calculator uses an approximate starting point of 35 milliliters of water per kilogram of body weight. Additional water is added for higher activity levels and warmer climates.

Example

A person weighing 70 kg would have a basic estimated water intake of approximately 2.45 liters per day before activity and climate adjustments.

Important Note

This calculator provides a general estimate for informational purposes. Individual hydration needs can vary. People with certain medical conditions or fluid restrictions should follow advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
