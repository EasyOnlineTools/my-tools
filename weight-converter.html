Understanding Weight and Mass Units

Metric units

Kilograms, grams, milligrams, and metric tonnes belong to the metric system. A kilogram is commonly used for body mass and household items, while grams and milligrams are useful for smaller quantities.

Imperial and US customary units

Pounds and ounces are widely used in the United States and other regions. Stones are commonly used for body weight in the UK. US short tons and UK long tons are different units used for larger quantities.

How does the calculation work?

The converter first changes the entered value to kilograms using a standard conversion factor. It then divides that mass by the conversion factor for the destination unit. This allows the tool to convert between any two supported units.
