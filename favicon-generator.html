What Is a Favicon?

A favicon is a small icon associated with a website. It can appear in browser tabs, bookmarks, browser history and shortcuts. A recognizable favicon helps visitors identify your website.

Why Are Different Favicon Sizes Needed?

Different browsers and devices may use different icon sizes. Small 16 × 16 and 32 × 32 icons are commonly used in browser tabs, while larger icons such as 180 × 180 and 192 × 192 are useful for mobile devices and shortcuts.
