What Is Pressure?

Pressure describes the force applied per unit area. It is measured in different units across science, engineering, weather reporting, industry and everyday applications. This converter uses Pascals as its common calculation base to convert between the listed units.

Common Pressure Units

Pascal (Pa)

The Pascal is the SI unit of pressure. One Pascal equals one newton per square meter.

Kilopascal (kPa) and Megapascal (MPa)

One kilopascal equals 1,000 Pascals, while one megapascal equals 1,000,000 Pascals. These units are useful in engineering and technical pressure measurements.

Bar and Millibar

One bar equals 100,000 Pascals. Millibars are commonly encountered in weather reporting; one millibar equals 100 Pascals.

Pounds per Square Inch (PSI)

PSI is widely used for tire pressure, hydraulic systems, compressors and other mechanical applications.

Atmosphere (atm)

The standard atmosphere is a reference unit equal to exactly 101,325 Pascals.

Millimeters of Mercury (mmHg) and Torr

These units are used in several pressure-measurement settings. They are very close in size but are not exactly identical under the conventional definitions used by this calculator.

Inches of Mercury and Inches of Water

Inches of mercury (inHg) and inches of water (inH₂O) are used in selected atmospheric, ventilation and pressure applications. Their conversion values depend on the adopted conventional reference definitions.

Pressure Conversion Formulas

The calculator first converts the entered value to Pascals and then divides by the conversion factor for the destination unit.

kPa to Pa: kPa × 1,000
MPa to Pa: MPa × 1,000,000
bar to Pa: bar × 100,000
atm to Pa: atm × 101,325
psi to Pa: psi × 6,894.757293168
Pa to kPa: Pa ÷ 1,000

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SI unit of pressure?

The Pascal (Pa) is the SI unit of pressure. One Pascal is equal to one newton per square meter.

How many Pascals are in one bar?

One bar equals exactly 100,000 Pascals, or 100 kilopascals.

How many Pascals are in one standard atmosphere?

One standard atmosphere equals exactly 101,325 Pascals.

Can I convert PSI to kPa?

Yes. Choose PSI as the source unit and kPa as the destination unit, enter your value and select Convert Pressure.

Can pressure be negative?

Negative values can be meaningful for gauge pressure or pressure differences relative to a reference. Negative absolute pressure is not physically valid, so interpret signed results according to the measurement context.

Is this pressure converter free?

Yes. This browser-based conversion tool is free to use.
