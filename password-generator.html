What is a Password Generator?

A password generator creates random passwords using a combination of letters, numbers and symbols. Strong passwords are generally harder for attackers to guess than short or predictable passwords.

Tips for Better Password Security

Use long, unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid using easily guessed information such as your name, birthday or common words. Consider using a reputable password manager to store unique passwords securely.
