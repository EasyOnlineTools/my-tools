How to Resize an Image

  1. Click Choose an Image and select a JPG, PNG or WebP file.
  2. Enter your required width and height in pixels.
  3. Keep Maintain Aspect Ratio selected to preserve image proportions.
  4. Choose your preferred output format.
  5. Click Resize Image and download the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. You can resize images for free without creating an account.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG and WebP images and choose one of these formats for the output.

Will resizing change the image quality?

Resizing changes the image dimensions. Enlarging a small image can reduce its apparent sharpness. JPG and WebP output may also use lossy compression.

What does Maintain Aspect Ratio mean?

It keeps the original width-to-height proportions so the image does not look stretched or distorted.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens directly in your browser.
